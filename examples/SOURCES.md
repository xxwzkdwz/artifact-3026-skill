# Example asset sources

All six gallery scenes were generated from text prompts with OpenAI's built-in image generation capability on 2026-09-02 for this repository. No stock photo, product photo, user upload, employer asset, logo, or third-party reference image was used. WANG ZHEN includes the generated scene images and derived cards under this repository's MIT license.

The local Owner trials under `output/` are excluded by `.gitignore` and are not source material for these examples.

| Example | Scene file | Prompt summary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meeting-room paper cup | `scenes/meeting-room-paper-cup.jpg` | A crushed, coffee-stained paper cup with fiber aging and a conservation support, viewed obliquely in a dim stone-and-metal vitrine; no branding or text. |
| Tangled charging cable | `scenes/tangled-charging-cable.jpg` | A generic knotted cable with cracked polymer, a trace of exposed copper and a conservation tie on a dark archive tray; no branded connector. |
| Forgotten folding umbrella | `scenes/forgotten-folding-umbrella.jpg` | A faded folded umbrella with restrained textile repairs and exposed steel ribs, recovered beside an imagined office doorway. |
| Worn wired earbuds | `scenes/worn-wired-earbuds.jpg` | Generic wired earbuds with uneven foam wear and a small cable repair in an intimate conservation alcove. |
| Faded brass key | `scenes/faded-brass-key.jpg` | A single generic house key with thumb polish and dark patina, photographed diagonally under grazing archive light. |
| Used pencil stub | `scenes/used-pencil-stub.jpg` | A short generic pencil with tooth marks, worn paint and a conservation cradle in an aged specimen drawer. |

Every prompt requested a photorealistic archaeological survivor, three-quarter or oblique framing, off-center placement, believable material aging, thick glass and restrained archive light. Every prompt excluded people, brands, logos, readable text, watermarks, bright sterile galleries, centered symmetry, product-photography polish, neon cyberpunk and identity-changing redesign.
